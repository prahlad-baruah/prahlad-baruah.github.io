About Me

I am a Physicist and Materials Scientist specializing in Plasmonic nanomaterials, Photocatalysis, Photoelectrocatalysis, and Laser-based nanoparticle synthesis. My research focuses on understanding and engineering nanoscale materials and interfaces using advanced spectroscopic and optical characterization techniques, with an emphasis on developing sustainable technologies for environmental remediation and pollutant degradation.

Research Interests

  • Laser-matter Interaction
  • Nanomaterial synthesis
  • Pulsed laser ablation in liquid
  • Plasmonics
  • Laser spectroscopy
